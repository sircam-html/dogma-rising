Your Account

No account yet? Create one in the launcher (see How To Play) and give it an e-mail address you can reach: that is where your password reset links go.

Forgot Your Password?

Enter the e-mail address of your account. We send you a link where you choose a new password.

  1. Open the link in the e-mail. Not in your inbox? Look in your spam folder and mark it as "Not spam".
  2. Type your account name and your new password.
  3. Log in with the new password in the launcher.

Old or wrong address on your account? Change your e-mail first.

Change Your E-mail

Reset links go to the e-mail address of your account. Enter your account name and password to set a new one.

Your old address gets a short notice about the change.
