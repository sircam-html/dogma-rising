The Road Ahead

Roadmap

Season 3 continues: a new chapter roughly every three months

  1. New Features

    Mid-December 2026Up next

    Clans, Pawns and Quests

    • Clan dungeons: find the lost diary books, then beat the clock to the treasure vault
    • Pawn Expeditions: your Partner Pawn sets out for the day and brings back finds
    • 77 world quests brought back across the land
    • Autoloot that stays on after you log out
    • /post: out in the field, your Partner Pawn takes your materials to storage, then comes back to your party

  2. Season 3.2

    Mid-December 2026Up next

    The Battle for the Royal Capital

    • 7 new main story quests for levels 89 to 94
    • Rally the troops and attack the Royal Capital
    • An Omen of Destruction, A Brief Dragon Force and The Plight of Lookout Castle
    • The Final Battle of the Royal Capital
    • Epitaph Road: Memory of Megadosys
    • New EX skills, unlocked through the Memory of Megadosys
    • Wild Hunts: track down B, A and S rank targets across the land

  3. Season 3.3

    Late February 2027

    The Urteca Mountains

    • 7 new main story quests for levels 95 to 100: the search for the missing prince
    • New region: the Urteca Mountains
    • The Royal Family Mausoleum and the Relics of the First King
    • Epitaph Road: Memory of Urteca
    • More EX skills, unlocked through the Memory of Urteca
    • Crown and Scepter: a side story in four parts

  4. Season 3.4

    Early May 2027

    The Fate of All

    • The story finale: 4 main quests at level 100
    • The Great Dragon Crystal Wars: 4 chain battles in lands shrouded in darkness
    • Crystal of Blessing missions: protect the crystal, or carry light to heal it with your party
    • Crystal War hard mode: after three sets, stronger foes and dark crystals guarded by minibosses
    • Dark-boss Extreme Missions, with an 8-player Restricted Stage
